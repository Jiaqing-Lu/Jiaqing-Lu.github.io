I am a faculty member at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. My research interests include all aspects of computational electromagnetics, such as finite element methods, domain decomposition methods, high-performance numerical algorithms, and electromagnetic simulation for integrated electronic systems.

I received my bachelor degree and master degree from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), and my Ph.D degree from The Ohio State University (OSU). During my Ph.D. study, I study at ElectroScience Laboratory (ESL) at OSU, and worked with Professor Jin-Fa Lee, one of the most influential authorities in computational electromagnetics and the key contributor to Ansys HFSS. After my graduation, I continued to work as a postdoc researcher at ESL. Then I went back to China, work as an algorithm engineer for software development at NineCube Electronics. Since 2026, I returned to SJTU and become a faculty member.
