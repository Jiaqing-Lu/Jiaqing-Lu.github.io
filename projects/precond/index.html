Related Publications

2024

  1. IEEE TMTT
    Convergence Enhancement for Embedded Domain Decomposition Method Through Spectral Radius Reduction
    Jiaqing Lu
    IEEE Transactions on Microwave Theory and Techniques, Oct 2024
    Abs HTML
    Domain Decomposition Preconditioning IC & EDA Antenna & Material

    The embedded domain decomposition method (DDM) has emerged as an innovative DDM variant for electromagnetic simulations, offering enhanced flexibility in geometrical modeling and mesh generation. While previous studies have shown rapid convergence for this method, our recent applications have uncovered certain convergence challenges. Targeting these convergence issues, this work introduces a preconditioner by constructing an approximate inverse to the system matrix of embedded DDM. The preconditioner is built upon two major ingredients: randomized sampling and spectral radius reduction. Through randomized sampling of system equations, we efficiently extract essential information from the DDM matrix without the need for explicit entry evaluations. These samples are then progressively compressed in an adaptive manner combined with spectral radius estimations. Numerical experiments demonstrate the impact of spectral radius on system convergence and the effectiveness of numerical compression in optimizing system eigenspectrum. Subsequent validation through numerical examples showcases the preconditioner’s capability in ensuring robust convergence and improving stability of embedded DDM.

  2. IEEE TMTT
    A Preconditioning Approach for the Domain Decomposition Simulation of High-Speed Circuits
    Jiaqing Lu
    IEEE Transactions on Microwave Theory and Techniques, Jun 2024
    Abs HTML
    Domain Decomposition Preconditioning IC & EDA

    Domain decomposition methods (DDMs) provide a powerful discretization framework for analyzing the electromagnetic (EM) phenomena in complex high-speed circuits. However, even with recent advancements in DDMs, achieving iterative convergence remains challenging for sophisticated structures. To facilitate the convergence of DDMs, we develop a preconditioner in this work, which is constructed by forming the approximate inverse of the DDM system matrix. The subdomains are recursively grouped into a multilevel tree, and randomized matrix compressions are adapted to handle their couplings. The approximate inverse preconditioner is then constituted by the inverses of the subdomain matrices with low-rank updates that account for their couplings. Through simulations of practical circuit examples, the strengths of the preconditioner are demonstrated by comparing it to a standard DDM solver. Importantly, a weakly convergent scenario is identified for DDMs when circuit signals propagate parallel to the subdomain interfaces. This situation, which significantly hinders computational efficiency and causes stability issues, can be effectively addressed through the proposed preconditioning approach. Finally, a novel integration is introduced to combine our method with a recently developed subdomain compression scheme.

  1. IEEE AWPL
    Convergence Improvement for Domain Decomposition Method Through Corner Treatment
    Jiaqing Lu and Jin-Fa Lee
    IEEE Antennas and Wireless Propagation Letters, Dec 2023
    Abs HTML
    Domain Decomposition Preconditioning Antenna & Material

    In complex electromagnetic simulations, domain decomposition method (DDM) has been shown to be an effective approach for discretization. However, convergence difficulty exists due to the redundant cement variables defined on the corner edges of the subdomains. In this work, we propose a solution to this issue by employing the method of Lagrange multiplier. By incorporating constraints for the corner fields and expanding the system matrix, the condition number of the DDM matrix is reduced significantly. As a result, the convergence rate of the DDM iterations is remarkably improved.

  1. IEEE EMC+SIPI
    Approximate Direct Inverse for Embedded Domain Decomposition Method
    Jiaqing Lu and Jin-Fa Lee
    In Proceedings of the IEEE International Symposium on Electromagnetic Compatibility, Signal and Power Integrity, Aug 2020
    Best EMC Symposium Paper Award – Honorable Mention
    Domain Decomposition Preconditioning EMC & Security

    The embedded domain decomposition method (DDM) is a powerful tool for analyzing complex electromagnetic applications. It can decompose a computational domain into nonconformal subdomains and provide great flexibility in modeling and optimizing add-on components. However, convergence issues are found for embedded DDM, particularly at low frequencies. To overcome the problem, we propose approximate direct inverse (ADI) based on the principle component analysis (PCA) of the system equations. The ADI is utilized as an effective preconditioner ensuring robust convergence of the embedded DDM system.
